# Paper rubric

Modelled on the questions conference reviewers answer. Use for conference, workshop and journal drafts, and for the abstract form (rows R1, R2, R4).

| Row | Criterion | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Contribution | Cannot be stated in one sentence | Stated, but its novelty is unclear | Clear and new; bulleted in the introduction | Clear, new, significant, and each bullet is a testable claim |
| R2 | Abstract and introduction | Missing moves (problem, gap, approach, result, implication) | All moves, result without a number | All moves with the main number | A reviewer could write the summary of the review from these alone |
| R3 | Motivation | Asserted | Cited from others' work | Measured | A measurement that makes the design look inevitable |
| R4 | Evaluation design | No baselines | Weak or untuned baselines | Fair baselines, stated questions | Fair, tuned baselines; questions first; ablations attribute every gain |
| R5 | Statistical care | Single runs, no variance | Variance for some results | Variance and run counts throughout | Variance, run counts and confidence stated; claims sized to the spread |
| R6 | Related work | Missing or a list of summaries | Grouped, differences unclear | Grouped, differences stated | Includes the closest competitor and says exactly how this work differs |
| R7 | Figures and tables | Unreadable in print | Descriptive captions | Takeaway captions | Each figure supports a named claim; readable in greyscale |
| R8 | Reproducibility | No details | Partial | Full setup and versions | Artifact available, or enough detail to rebuild the main result |
| R9 | Limitations | None | Generic | Specific | Specific, with threats to validity and when the result would not hold |
| R10 | Sentences and words | Hard to read aloud | Frequent lapses | Mostly clear | Clear, consistent terms; every symbol and acronym defined |
