# White paper rubric

| Row | Criterion | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Decision and readers | No decision named | Decision implied | Decision and decision-maker named | Decision, decision-maker and technical lead each served by the parts written for them |
| R2 | Executive summary | Missing or longer than one page | Describes the paper instead of the recommendation | Problem, recommendation and benefit | Five-sentence summary readable alone: problem, recommendation, benefit with a number, cost, risk |
| R3 | Options | One option | Straw-man alternatives | Real alternatives including doing nothing | Alternatives steelmanned and compared on the same criteria |
| R4 | Evidence | Vendor claims or none | Generic benchmarks | Measurements on the reader's workload | Reader's workload, fair baselines, variance, methods in an appendix |
| R5 | Cost | Not mentioned | Speed only | Cost stated | Cost per unit of work, compared across options |
| R6 | Risks | None | Listed without mitigation | Risks with mitigations | Risks that could reverse the recommendation, with what to watch |
| R7 | Plan | None | Vague next steps | Steps and owners | Steps, owners, duration, success measure |
| R8 | Sentences and words | Hard to read aloud | Jargon in the summary | Mostly clear | Summary free of jargon; evidence sections precise |
