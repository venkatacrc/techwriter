# General rubric

Used for any piece without a form-specific rubric, and as the first five rows of every other rubric. Score each row 1–4. A 4 means a demanding reader would find nothing to fix on that row; give it rarely. Quote the text that justifies each score: a score without evidence is a mood.

| Row | Criterion | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Reader and purpose | No identifiable reader | Reader implied but text drifts between audiences | One reader, served throughout, with lapses | Every paragraph serves the named reader's decision |
| R2 | Claim | Topic only, no claim | Claim present but vague or buried | Claim specific and early | Claim specific, testable, right-sized, in the first paragraph |
| R3 | Structure | Chronological or random order | Some grouping; reader must reorganise | Reader's order; reverse outline mostly clean | Reverse outline reads as a complete argument |
| R4 | Evidence | Assertions without numbers | Numbers without baseline or conditions | Numbers with baselines and conditions | Every claim traced to evidence of fitting strength, including variance |
| R5 | Sentences and words | Hard to read aloud; lint fix rate above 10 per 1000 words | Frequent nominalisations, long sentences, filler | Mostly clear; occasional lapses | Clear, concrete, consistent terms; lint clean or every exception justified |
