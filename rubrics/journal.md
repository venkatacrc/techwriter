# Journal article rubric

Use the paper rubric (`rubrics/paper.md`) plus these rows. For a draft whose kind is `paper` but is headed to a journal, score these rows by hand at the end of the review sheet.

| Row | Criterion | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Completeness | Conference length and depth | Some extension | Full method and extended experiments | The definitive account: nothing a reader would need is elsewhere |
| R2 | New material over prior version | None | Minor | Substantial and described | Substantial, described in the cover letter, and meets the journal's policy |
| R3 | Discussion | None | Restates results | Implications and limitations | Implications, limitations and threats to validity, specific |
| R4 | Response letter (revisions) | Comments ignored | Some addressed vaguely | All addressed | All addressed point by point with locations and quoted changes |
