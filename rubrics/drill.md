# Drill rubric

Drills are scored against their own **Done when** criteria (in `drills/catalog.md`), not against general quality. Most drills need no review sheet; mark them done with `python tools/tw.py done FILE --minutes N`. Review a drill only if you repeat it, to compare the two attempts.

| Row | Criterion | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Done-when criteria met | None | Some | All, with effort visible | All, and the result would survive a demanding reader |
| R2 | Within the time box | Far over or abandoned | Over | On time | On time with a check pass |
| R3 | Skill transferred | Not yet applied elsewhere | Applied once | Applied in this week's piece | Applied without thinking about it |
