# Postmortem rubric

| Row | Criterion | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Summary | Missing or narrative | Present but omits impact or cause | What broke, impact and cause in a few sentences | Readable alone; a manager and an engineer both get what they need |
| R2 | Impact | Not stated | Qualitative ("some jobs failed") | Measured | Measured, with duration and who was affected |
| R3 | Timeline | Missing | Actions only | Observations, hypotheses and actions | Includes the wrong hypotheses and the evidence that disproved each |
| R4 | Mechanism | Symptom named as cause | Cause named, not explained | Mechanism explained | Explained well enough that a reader could have predicted the failure, with a minimal reproducer |
| R5 | Blamelessness | Blames a person | Neutral but focused on individuals' choices | Focused on systems | Shows why each decision was reasonable at the time |
| R6 | Action items | None | Vague ("be more careful") | Specific | Specific, owned, dated, each tied to a contributing factor |
| R7 | Sentences and words | Hard to read aloud | Jargon and long sentences | Mostly clear | Clear to an engineer from another team |
