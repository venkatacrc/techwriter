# Journal

Your writing lives here, one folder per year, one file per piece: `YYYY-MM-DD-KIND-SLUG.md`. Review sheets sit next to the piece they review (`*.review.md`).

Keep everything, including bad drafts. Rereading a drill from week 1 in week 12 is the clearest measure of progress you will get.

Pieces under employer review or containing unpublished results may need to stay private. Keep this repository private, or keep those files out of it.
